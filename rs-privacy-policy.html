RoadSound

Privacy Policy

This page explains how RoadSound handles information when you use the app, including ads, purchases, third-party embeds, and device permissions.

Effective May 25, 2026
App RoadSound
Package com.roadsound.app

Summary

RoadSound is built for ambient audio and music playback. The app does not require an account for basic use, and it does not request microphone access. Some features rely on third-party services for ads, purchases, or embedded media playback.

What we collect directly

RoadSound does not collect personal information directly for sign-in or profile management.

  • We do not ask for your contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • We do not request microphone access.
  • We do not run a first-party account system.

Information handled by the app

  • Device and app information such as OS version, app version, and device identifiers.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data when ads are shown.
  • Purchase history, restore status, and entitlement state for premium access.
  • Playback requests sent to SoundCloud or YouTube when you use those features.
  • Diagnostic and crash data generated by the device, OS, or third-party providers.

Third-party services

RoadSound uses the following services to operate the app:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner advertising.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases and entitlement checks.
  • SoundCloud and YouTube embeds when you use those players inside the app.

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies, including device identifiers, IP address, purchase information, and usage data.

How the information is used

We use information to:

  • Play audio and keep the session active.
  • Show ads where applicable.
  • Verify premium purchases and restore purchases.
  • Maintain app functionality and platform compatibility.

Permissions

The app may request permissions such as wake lock, audio settings, or screen brightness control so playback can continue and the screen can dim during sleep sessions. These permissions are used locally on your device and are not intended to collect personal data.

Children

RoadSound is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information listed on the Google Play Store or App Store listing for RoadSound.
